2. Description of the service

Learn Dutch: Speak NL is a language learning app for iOS and Android that teaches Dutch as spoken in the Netherlands through:

  • short lessons set in everyday Amsterdam situations, currently covering the beginner level (CEFR A1)
  • listening, reading, matching and speaking exercises with explanations
  • grammar notes and spaced-repetition reviews
  • daily goals, streaks, an energy system and two companion characters
  • an optional premium subscription

Lessons are bundled with the App. An internet connection is needed for speaking feedback, ads and subscription management. We may add, change or remove content over time.

8. Speech recognition and learning content

Pronunciation feedback is an approximate learning aid. Speech recognition can be wrong, especially with background noise, strong accents or very short recordings.

  • Scores are guides, not formal assessments.
  • Pronunciation hints are simplified spellings meant to help beginners, not exact phonetic transcriptions.
