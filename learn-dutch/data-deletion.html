Data Deletion Guide

Learn Dutch: Speak NL — How to clear or delete your data

Learn Dutch: Speak NL has no accounts and no cloud sync. Your learning data lives on your device, so you are always in control of it, and we have no copy to delete.

What is stored where

On your device only:

  • lesson progress, learned words and phrases, phrase strength and review schedule
  • streak, daily statistics, energy, coins and daily goal
  • preferences: app language, companion, display name, reminder settings and onboarding answers
  • premium status mirrored from RevenueCat

With third parties:

  • RevenueCat: subscription and purchase history, if you subscribed
  • Google AdMob: advertising data, if you use the free version
  • Voice clips: processed in real time and not stored by us. Nothing to delete.

Option A: Reset your progress in the App

  1. Open the Progress tab.
  2. Scroll to the Profile section and tap Reset journey.
  3. Confirm with Reset. Lesson progress, phrase strength, streak, coins and today's goal are cleared and you start again from lesson 1.

Option B: Clear all App data on Android

  1. Open Settings > Apps.
  2. Tap Learn Dutch: Speak NL > Storage & cache.
  3. Tap Clear storage and confirm. The App stays installed and starts fresh.

Option C: Delete the App (iOS and Android)

This is permanent. All progress, streak, coins and settings are removed and cannot be recovered. A subscription is not cancelled by deleting the App; you can restore it after reinstalling.

  1. iOS: press and hold the App icon, tap Remove App, then Delete App.
  2. Android: press and hold the App icon and tap Uninstall, or use Settings > Apps > Learn Dutch: Speak NL > Uninstall.

Delete subscription data at RevenueCat

Email bora.nikolai.mindwise@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". Tell us the platform and roughly when you subscribed. We forward the request to RevenueCat and confirm within 30 days. Apple and Google keep their own purchase records under their policies.

Advertising choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: open the Progress tab and tap Privacy choices to change your ad consent choices.
  • Everywhere: the App requests non-personalised ads only.
  • iOS: the App does not request tracking permission, so the iOS advertising identifier is not used.
  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads) > Delete advertising ID.

Frequently asked questions

Does deleting my data cancel my subscription?

No. Cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. After reinstalling, tap Restore purchases in the Progress tab.

Can I get my progress back after deleting it?

No. We have no backup because your progress never leaves your device.

Are my voice recordings stored anywhere?

No. Clips are processed in real time and discarded. Nothing stays on our servers or on your device after the exercise.

Can I get a copy of my data?

Email us with the subject "Data Export Request". The only server-side data linked to the App is your RevenueCat subscription history, which we can request for you.
