1. Introduction

Welcome to Learn Dutch: Speak NL (the "App", "we", "us" or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, why, who else processes it, and the rights you have.

The App is private by default: your learning progress lives on your device, there is no account to create, and we do not keep a profile of you on any server.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Data stored only on your device

The App stores its data on your device with React Native AsyncStorage under the keys learn_dutch_progress_v1, learn_dutch_notification_runtime_v1 and speechConsentAt. We never receive a copy. It includes:

  • Learning progress: completed lessons, words and phrases you have learned, phrase strength, review schedule and recent mistakes
  • Streak and study history: study days, last study date and daily statistics
  • App state: energy, coins, daily goal progress and smart reviews used today
  • Preferences and onboarding answers: your app language, companion, the display name you choose, daily goal, reminder settings and the reason you gave for learning Dutch
  • Subscription status: premium flag, plan type and expiry date, mirrored from RevenueCat
  • Reminder state: which local reminders are scheduled and when one was last shown
  • Speaking notice: the date you accepted the speaking-practice notice described in section 3

Deleting the App, or using Reset journey in the App, removes this data. Reset journey clears lesson progress, phrase strength, streak, coins and today's goal.

2.2 Audio from speaking exercises

When you do a speaking exercise, the App records a short clip with your microphone. The clip is:

  • sent over HTTPS to our speech service, a Supabase Edge Function
  • forwarded by that service to OpenAI's audio transcription API, which turns it into text; only the audio and a neutral instruction go to OpenAI, and our service then compares the text with the Dutch phrase
  • not stored, logged or kept by us once the result is returned

OpenAI processes the audio as our sub-processor under its API terms. Audio sent through the API is not used to train OpenAI's models, and OpenAI keeps it only for a limited period for abuse monitoring. See OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

2.3 Speech service rate limiting

To protect the speech service from abuse, it keeps a request counter per client for one-minute and one-day windows, plus one total daily count for the whole service. The per-client counter is keyed by a one-way SHA-256 hash of the network address the request came from. It holds no audio, no text, no readable IP address and nothing that identifies you.

2.4 Subscription and purchase data (RevenueCat)

If you buy a premium subscription, Apple or Google processes the payment and RevenueCat manages the subscription. RevenueCat may process:

  • an anonymous app user identifier generated on your device
  • purchase transaction identifiers, product and price
  • subscription status, trial status and entitlement data

We never receive your payment card details. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

2.5 Advertising data (Google AdMob, free users)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google may process:

  • device identifiers (on Android the advertising ID; on iOS an identifier for vendors, because the App does not request tracking permission and the iOS advertising identifier is not available)
  • IP address, which Google may use to estimate a general location
  • ad interactions such as impressions and taps
  • device information such as model and operating system version, plus diagnostic and performance data of the ads SDK

The App asks Google for non-personalised ads only, so your interests and activity in other apps are not used to choose the ads you see. Premium subscribers do not see ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

2.6 Data we do not collect

  • Name, email address, phone number or account credentials (there is no sign-in)
  • Precise location
  • Contacts, calendar, photos or files
  • Stored voice recordings
  • Analytics or behavioural tracking of our own

3. Microphone and audio

  • Before your first speaking exercise, the App explains that your short recording is sent to our server and transcribed by OpenAI, and that we do not keep it. Recording starts only after you continue.
  • The App asks for microphone access only for speaking exercises.
  • The microphone records only while you hold or tap the record control in a speaking exercise. It never listens in the background.
  • Recordings travel over encrypted HTTPS and are used only to produce your pronunciation feedback.
  • You can withdraw microphone access at any time in your device settings and skip speaking exercises; the rest of the App keeps working.

5. Legal bases for processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on:

  • Contract: providing the learning service and subscriptions you ask for.
  • Consent: microphone access, notifications, and storing or reading information on your device for ads. You can withdraw consent at any time in the App or in your device settings.
  • Legitimate interests: preventing abuse of the speech service and showing non-personalised ads that keep the free version free.
  • Legal obligation: complying with applicable law.

6. Speech processing details

What the App sends:

  • the audio clip (base64 encoded, usually 2 to 10 seconds)
  • the Dutch phrase you were asked to say (for example "Goedemorgen") and its pronunciation hint
  • the language code (nl), the audio format and the phrase's internal identifier

What goes to OpenAI: only the audio clip and a neutral instruction ("Dutch speech from a language learner"). The phrase text stays on our server, where it is used to score the transcript.

What comes back: a transcript, a similarity score and normalised text.

What we keep: nothing from the request. The Edge Function processes it in memory. The only record is the anonymous rate-limit counter described in section 2.3.

All traffic to the speech service and to OpenAI uses HTTPS with TLS encryption.

7. Reminders

Study reminders are scheduled on your device as local notifications. The App does not register for remote push notifications and sends no device token to us. You can turn reminders off in the App or in your device settings.

During a free trial, study reminders pause. Instead, if notifications are allowed, the App schedules one reminder about a day before the cancellation deadline, which is 24 hours before the trial ends, so you can decide whether to keep the subscription. Study reminders resume after the trial, also if you do not open the App.

8. Data storage and security

  • On your device: stored in the App's sandboxed storage and protected by your device's security, including encryption at rest on current iOS and Android versions.
  • Speech service (Supabase): requests travel over TLS; audio is not persisted. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • Subscriptions (RevenueCat): stored on RevenueCat's servers with industry-standard encryption.
  • Ads (Google AdMob): handled on Google's infrastructure.

No system is completely secure. If you find a security issue, please email us right away.

9. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. Data is shared only with:

  • Supabase, which hosts the speech service (processor; audio not retained)
  • OpenAI, which transcribes speaking clips (sub-processor; not used for training)
  • RevenueCat, which manages subscriptions (processor)
  • Google AdMob, which serves ads to free users (independent controller for advertising data)
  • Apple and Google, which distribute the App and process in-app purchases
  • authorities, where the law requires it, or a successor in the event of a merger or acquisition, in which case you will be told in advance

10. Data retention

  • Data on your device: until you delete the App or use Reset journey. We have no copy.
  • Speech audio: not retained by us; discarded as soon as the result is returned. OpenAI keeps API data only for a limited period for abuse monitoring.
  • Rate-limit counters: deleted after at most 30 days.
  • RevenueCat: for the life of your subscription plus RevenueCat's legal retention period.
  • Google AdMob: according to Google's retention policies.

14. Advertising, consent and tracking

The App uses no cookies and no tracking of its own. Advertising works as follows:

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ad is requested, Google's consent message asks how Google and its partners may store and use information for ads, for example to measure ad performance. You can change your choices at any time with Privacy choices in the Progress tab.
  • Everywhere: the App requests non-personalised ads only. Ads are chosen from context such as the App itself, not from a profile of you.
  • iOS: the App does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, so the iOS advertising identifier is not shared and the App does not track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

The App does not respond to browser Do-Not-Track signals because it is not a website; use the controls above instead.
